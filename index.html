Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get to the Bok building?

There are several ways to get there!

Shuttle: We will be providing shuttles from 30th Street Station and Rittenhouse Square. All information about the shuttles is included in the email we sent out on January 6. Please contact us if you have any further questions or didn't receive the email.

Bus: The 47 bus (and its much less frequent cousin, the 47M) goes straight down 8th Street, stopping right by Bok at 8th and Mifflin. Click here for map and schedule; we also recommend using the SEPTA travel planner here If you are coming from 30th Street Station, you can take the 9 bus from 30th and Market (right outside the station) to 8th and Chestnut, where you can transfer (across Chestnut Street) to the southbound 47. All SEPTA modes of transit allow payment by tapping a credit card.

Train: We recommend people coming from points on the Northeast Corridor to take the train to 30th Street Station in Philadelphia and then the shuttle or bus (see above). For those coming from New York and other points north, we recommend Amtrak trains 155 and 663, both of which arrive in Philadelphia around 10:30.

Car: We really do not recommend driving all the way to Bok, which does not have dedicated parking. If you wish to avoid having to find street parking, which is difficult at the best of times and all but impossible in case of snow or an Eagles home playoff game (both of which are possible on a Sunday in January), we recommend parking in one of the several lots by 30th Street Station and taking the shuttle or bus (see above).

If you are struggling with navigating public transit options, please email us at hannahplusguy@gmail.com.

Where should I stay?

Since Bok is located in a residential neighborhood, we would not recommend staying nearby. Instead, we would recommend staying in Center City, which has many more hotels and things to do. We will be staying in a hotel near Rittenhouse Square, by the location of any potential Center City shuttle pickup location (see above). If you do choose to stay outside of Center City, you may consider: Queen Village, Passyunk, Northern Liberties, Fishtown, Brewerytown. If you are unfamiliar with Philadelphia neighborhoods, feel free to consult with us about safety and transit connectivity of potential locations at hannahplusguy@gmail.com.

What else can I do while I’m in Philadelphia?

Philadelphia is a wonderful and beautiful city with many world-class museums, including the Franklin Institute (very child-friendly!), the Barnes Foundation, the Philadelphia Museum of Art, and the Academy of Natural Sciences, just to name a few. We also recommend walking around and taking in the lovely architecture of the houses along the side streets of Center City. Reading Terminal Market has great local food stalls (Bassett’s ice cream, Beiler’s donuts, and Amish soft pretzels in particular). We are happy to give more tailored recommendations upon request!

What health precautions should I take?

We know that the winter season often comes with heightened spread of illness, and we encourage everyone to take precautions they feel comfortable with in the period before the wedding, including travel. If you are sick, please stay home; if you are concerned about communicating or catching something, we will have some masks available.

What’s the dress code?

Cocktail attire. Does that mean anything? Wear something nice that you feel good in, and make sure you can dance because we expect a lively hora (circle dance) after the ceremony! The venue has requested that guests not wear stilettos and other shoes that may damage the floor. If you want more specific direction, feel free to consult with us. The dress code for drinks the night before is casual.

Should I take photographs?

During the ceremony and subsequent dancing, we encourage you to let the photographers do their jobs and focus on enjoying yourself. If you take any good photos during the eating portions of the day, feel free to send them to us at hannahplusguy@gmail.com!

Is there a registry?

No—we will be moving abroad one week after the wedding and will not be able to bring additional physical items with us. If you wish to give us a gift (optional, really!!), you may send us money via Zelle (both of us are registered under our emails and our American phone numbers), check, or your preferred method and we will use that money to buy dishes, a very fancy stand mixer, and other household items after we arrive in Brno.
