Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get to the Bok building?

There are several ways to get there!

Shuttle: We will be providing a shuttle from 30th Street Station and/or Center City (probably near Rittenhouse Square), based on indicated interest in the RSVP form.

Bus: The 47 bus (and its much less frequent cousin, the 47M) goes straight down 8th Street, stopping right by Bok at 8th and Mifflin. Click here for map and schedule. If you are coming from 30th Street Station, you can take the 9 bus from 30th and Market (right outside the station) to 8th and Chestnut, where you can transfer (across Chestnut Street) to the southbound 47.

Train: We recommend people coming from points on the Northeast Corridor to take the train to Philadelphia and then the shuttle or bus (see above).

Car: We really do not recommend driving all the way to Bok, which does not have dedicated parking. If you wish to avoid having to find street parking, which is difficult at the best of times and all but impossible in case of snow or an Eagles home playoff game (both of which are possible on a Sunday in January).

If you are struggling with navigating public transit options, please email us at hannahplusguy@gmail.com.

Where should I stay?

Since Bok is located in a residential neighborhood, we would not recommend staying nearby. Instead, we would recommend staying in Center City, which has many more hotels. We will be staying in a hotel somewhere around Rittenhouse Square, near the location of the aufruf and any potential Center City shuttle pickup location (see above).

What’s the dress code?

Cocktail attire. Does that mean anything? Wear something nice that you feel good in! If you’re unsure, feel free to check in with us.

No—we will be moving abroad one week after the wedding and will not be able to bring additional physical items with us. If you wish to give us a gift (optional, really!!), you may send us money via Zelle, check, or your preferred method and we will use that money to buy dishes, a very fancy stand mixer, and other household items after we arrive in Brno.

What is an aufruf? What is a kiddush?

An aufruf is a special honor given to the spouses-to-be during synagogue services on the Shabbat before their wedding. It is an occasion to symbolically announce and celebrate the upcoming event to and with the community. Kiddush is the blessing over wine said at the start of meals, and in a general sense is also used to refer to the snacks provided after Saturday morning services.

What else can I do while I’m in Philadelphia?

Philadelphia is a wonderful and beautiful city with many world-class museums, including the Franklin Institute (very child-friendly!), the Barnes Foundation, the Philadelphia Museum of Art, and the Academy of Natural Sciences, just to name a few. We also recommend walking around and taking in the lovely architecture of the houses along the side streets of Center City. Reading Terminal Market has great local food stalls (Bassett’s ice cream, Beiler’s donuts, and Amish soft pretzels in particular). We are happy to give more tailored recommendations upon request!
